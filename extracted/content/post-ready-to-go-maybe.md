## Ready to go?

Jul 16, 2021

1 min read

We are less than 36 hours from our flight and awaiting anxiously for the results of the COVID-19 PCR test. In our brave new world, it seems that even a road trip cannot begin until the pandemic gives it a green light. The chances for a positive result are slim but it still seems to add unnecessary stress and reduce the sense of freedom which is supposed to accompany such a trip. Alas, all that we can do is wait.
